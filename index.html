What's tracked, and why — the workload figures and burnout score on this page are simulated for this prototype. In production, this panel is populated by your on-device activity signal (Microsoft 365 apps, browser activity, screen & input activity), leave records and attendance data, feeding the wellbeing agent, under the organization's monitoring policy you've been notified of. The score exists to prompt a private, supportive conversation between you, your supervisor and HR — it is never used as an input to performance reviews or disciplinary decisions. Questions about what's collected or how it's used can go to your HR Partner above.
